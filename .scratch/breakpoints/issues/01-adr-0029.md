# ADR 0029: breakpoints

Status: resolved
Type: task

## What

`docs/adr/0029-breakpoints.md`, recording the spec's decisions:
- any number of breakpoints, set by clicking a line number;
- the interpreter checks them through `Control`, and waits on a breakpoint line even when not in lockstep, so Instant stops exactly;
- a breakpoint stops where a line starts, as `starts_line` does;
- replays ignore them until they get back to where you paused;
- only the editor's program stops at them;
- they aren't saved;
- they follow their lines through edits;
- no message when one stops Kame.

ADR 0012 (the highlight and stepping) gets a line pointing at it, where it says only Instant leaves lockstep off.

The ADR is written, and 0012 points at it.
