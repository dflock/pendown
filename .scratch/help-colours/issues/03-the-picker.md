# The colour picker

Status: done
Type: task
Blocked by: 01, 02

## What

On the Colours page, under "Find a colour":

- the hue and saturation wheel, drawn as a picture for the current brightness and kept, with a marker over it; click or drag on it;
- brightness and opacity bars, each showing its range for the current colour, with a handle; click or drag on them;
- R, G, B, A and hex fields that follow the wheel and bars, and set them when typed in; a field that doesn't make sense is outlined, with the reason under it;
- it starts at Kame's pen colour, from the last `Watch`.

## Done when

The picker works as in the mockups, and screenshots show it.
