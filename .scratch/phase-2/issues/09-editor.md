Status: ready-for-agent
Type: task

## What

- `{` and `}` colour as brackets, `?`, `?1` and `?REST` as commands.
- The instruction lists of `RUN`, `FOR`, `WHILE`, `UNTIL`, `DO.WHILE`, `DO.UNTIL`, `CATCH` and `IGNORE` are checked while typing, as `REPEAT`'s are.
